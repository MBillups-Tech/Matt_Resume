Job Duties:

Architect high-throughput solutions that power our most critical operations, ensuring scalability and efficiency
Expand and enhance our self-service platform, collaborating with cross-functional teams to fuel our AI, ML, and analytics goals
Tackle complex distributed data challenges, streamline system integrations, and uphold high standards of quality and governance
Champion cutting-edge technologies, keeping our platform at the forefront of industry advancements and enabling strategic outcomes

Perform data modeling, data access, and data replication techniques, such as CDC
Architect applications at scale and maintain infrastructure as code via Terraform.
Telecommuting: 100% Remote; Must reside in U.S.; Reports to HQ in New York, NY.

Bachelor’s degree or U.S. equivalent in Computer Science, Computer Engineering, or a related field and 5 years of professional experience as a Software Engineer, Systems Engineer, or any occupation, job title, or position involving design, development, and implementation of scalable distributed systems, data platforms, and high-throughput software applications.

Must also have the following experience:

5 years of professional experience developing object-oriented applications
5 years of professional experience troubleshooting application and infrastructure performance issues in distributed computing environments
5 years of professional experience evaluating system architecture and engineering tradeoffs related to performance, scalability, reliability, and maintainability
3 years of professional experience designing and supporting data warehouse and query engine technologies including Snowflake or Trino
3 years of professional experience designing and implementing data storage, data modeling, and data replication solutions utilizing Apache Iceberg, Parquet, Arrow, Hudi, or change data capture (CDC)
3 years of professional experience designing and supporting distributed data processing systems utilizing Spark, Flink, or Kafka
3 years of professional experience designing, implementing, and optimizing distributed systems to support high-throughput, scalable, and reliable data processing 
1 year of professional experience developing and supporting cloud-based infrastructure utilizing AWS, Kubernetes, and Infrastructure-as-Code tools, including Terraform
