Overview:

Overview
The Senior Data Platform Operations Engineer builds and operates reliable, secure, automated services that support enterprise data integration, metadata, governance, deployment, and hybrid connectivity. The position focuses on DataHub, Fivetran Hybrid, AKS-dependent services, self-hosted integration runtimes, production deployment automation, monitoring, and the shared operational interfaces among EDS, Cloud, DevOps, and application teams.


Education
Bachelor's Degree (Preferred)
or an equivalent combination of education and relevant professional experience that demonstrates the required competencies.
Certification/Licensure
Relevant Azure, Kubernetes, Terraform, DevOps, or data-integration certification. (Preferred)


Experience
Experience supporting and troubleshooting production cloud, DevOps, container, integration, and infrastructure platforms within complex enterprise environments.
Hands-on experience with Kubernetes or Azure Kubernetes Service (AKS), including deployments, monitoring, connectivity, incident resolution, and container management.
Experience automating platform operations using Terraform or other Infrastructure as Code (IaC), CI/CD pipelines, PowerShell, Python, Bash, APIs, or command-line tools.
Working knowledge of Azure cloud infrastructure, including identity, networking, secrets management, security, monitoring, capacity, recovery, and hybrid cloud connectivity.
Experience supporting hybrid data integration and production environments, collaborating across Cloud, DevOps, Security, Infrastructure, and application teams; experience within healthcare or another regulated environment preferred.
