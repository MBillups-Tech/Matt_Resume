Description
Team Overview
Reporting to the People Analytics BI Enablement Leader with the People Analytics organization, the HR Data Engineer along with peers enable the People Analytics business intelligence (BI) strategy. This role collaborates across the BI Enablement team and with partner groups to assemble large datasets, build clear metric definitions, integrate data, certify data products, and scale actionable insights using Databricks technology.

What You’ll Do

Define, communicate, and maintain master data, metadata, data management repositories, logical data models, and data standards.
Design, build, and maintain scalable, reliable data pipeline architecture.
Assemble and transform large, complex datasets to meet functional and non-functional business requirements.
Build and maintain consistent, accurate analytical datasets for business intelligence and data science users.
Develop industrialized analytical datasets and delivery mechanisms that turn data into actionable insights across workforce, talent acquisition, operational efficiency, and other business performance areas.
Partner with business stakeholders to resolve data-related technical issues and define requirements for their data infrastructure needs.
Identify and implement process improvements, including automating manual work, optimizing data delivery, and redesigning infrastructure to improve scalability.
Enable advanced analytics by making trusted, well-documented data products easier to discover and use.


Required Qualifications

2+ years of hands-on experience with big data technologies such as Hadoop, Spark, or Kafka.
Advanced SQL skills and experience with relational and NoSQL databases, including technologies such as PostgreSQL and Cassandra.
2+ years of experience with an object-oriented or functional programming language, such as Python, Java, C++, or Scala.
Experience with data wrangling and preparation tools such as Alteryx, Trifacta, SAS, or Datameer.
Familiarity with stream-processing systems such as Storm or Spark Streaming.
Ability to diagnose and solve problems quickly and thoroughly.
Ability to identify repetitive or manual tasks and automate them.
Understanding of networking and distributed computing concepts.
Strong organization and prioritization skills, with the ability to manage multiple tasks in a dynamic environment.


Preferred Qualifications

Experience with data pipeline and workflow orchestration tools such as Azkaban, Luigi, or Apache Airflow.
Experience with AWS cloud services such as EC2, EMR, RDS, or Redshift.
