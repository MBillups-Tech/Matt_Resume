What will YOU be doing for us?   Independently design, develop, test, deploy, and maintain modern data warehouse solutions that transform replicated SKYGEN Enterprise System application data into trusted, reusable data products for analytics and reporting. This role uses Snowflake and dbt to build scalable dimensional models, apply data-quality controls, analyze complex source-system data and relationships, and maintain clear business and technical lineage across SKYGEN’s enterprise data environment supporting more than 160 client databases.

What will YOU be working on every day? 

Data Warehouse Design & Dimensional Modeling: Independently design, develop, deploy, and maintain dimensional data warehouse objects, including fact, dimension, bridge, and reference tables, using approved Kimball and relational modeling standards. Define grain, business and surrogate keys, historical tracking, slowly changing dimensions, effective dating, and referential relationships for assigned subject areas.
dbt Data Development: Develop modular, reusable, documented, and testable dbt models that transform replicated source data through standardized staging, integration, dimensional, and publication layers. Use appropriate dbt sources, references, tests, macros, snapshots, and incremental processing patterns.
Landed Data Usage: Use replicated and ingested source data made available in Snowflake to build downstream transformation and publication layers. Validate the completeness, availability, and usability of source data without owning ingestion-platform infrastructure.
Enterprise Data Analysis: Analyze complex SKYGEN Enterprise System application schemas to identify authoritative data elements, keys, relationships, business rules, and historical behavior required to support defined reporting, analytics, and data-product use cases.
Data Quality & Reconciliation: Develop automated validation and reconciliation controls for completeness, accuracy, uniqueness, accepted values, referential integrity, historical consistency, and source-to-target transformation results. Investigate exceptions and document resolution.
Lineage & Documentation: Maintain source-to-target mappings, transformation logic, model and column descriptions, dependencies, lineage, data-quality rules, assumptions, and known limitations so warehouse objects remain discoverable and understandable.
Snowflake Development & Optimization: Develop and optimize Snowflake objects and queries with consideration for performance, scalability, concurrency, maintainability, and efficient resource use. Use query history and query profiles to support troubleshooting and optimization.
Software Development Lifecycle: Apply software engineering practices to data development, including Git-based source control, peer review, branching and pull-request workflows, automated testing, CI/CD, release validation, deployment documentation, and rollback planning.
Monitor & Support Solutions: Monitor data transformations and published data products, investigate failures and discrepancies, perform root-cause analysis, implement sustainable corrections, and communicate status and technical findings to internal and external stakeholders.
Collaboration: Work with data integration, analytics, reporting, quality assurance, platform, security, and business teams to translate approved requirements into reliable production data solutions.
