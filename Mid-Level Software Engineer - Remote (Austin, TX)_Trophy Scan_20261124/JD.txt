Job description
About TrophyScan
TrophyScan is a new platform built for outdoor enthusiasts to showcase their achievements, compete in virtual competitions, and preserve 3D-scanned replicas of their greatest trophies. Think Strava for hunting. Using AI and advanced computer vision, our app captures precise measurements of animals, racks, and other trophies, creating a digital 3D twin and powering a data-driven leaderboard that recognizes real skill and accomplishment in the field.

We’re a small, close-knit team that ships every week. We work remote, meet up in person a couple of times a month, and have a good time building things nobody’s built before.

The Role
You’ll work directly with our Head of Engineering. You’ll own meaningful parts of the product (Flutter mobile app, React web apps, Python API, or the C++ 3D and scoring pipeline) and ship production-ready work with high autonomy and code review. You’ll expand across more of the stack, including AWS infrastructure, and help shape how we build. You’ll also partner with our product team (who build with AI agents) and turn their work into reliable, tested systems. As the team grows, you’ll help mentor junior engineers.

What You Bring
A degree in computer science, math, physics, engineering, or a related field, or equivalent experience
Strong hands-on experience with several of the following: Flutter, Dart, React, TypeScript, Python, FastAPI, C++, PostgreSQL, Firebase, AWS (EC2, S3, SQS), Linux, Terraform, Packer, CI/CD
Solid fundamentals in data structures, algorithms, object-oriented programming, and networking
Proven experience with testing, error handling, error reporting, and production reliability
Strong instincts for readable, maintainable, and well-structured code
Multiple things you’ve shipped (ideally in production)
Experience with AI coding tools and the judgment to review, correct, and own what they produce
Solid understanding of Git and collaborative development practices
Ability to stay effective and keep moving when the path isn’t obvious
Nice to Have
Hunting or fishing enthusiast
Mobile app development, including shipping to the App Store and Google Play
Enthusiasm for math, physics, or engineering
Experience with 3D graphics or computer vision
Hands-on ML experience: segmentation, keypoints, object detection
Experience mentoring or reviewing junior engineers
What You’ll Work With
You’ll work across the full stack, from mobile and web to APIs, 3D and computer vision, and cloud infrastructure, and help design, build, and maintain a production system end to end.

How You’ll Grow
First week: get set up and ship your first meaningful change
First 90 days: take clear ownership of one or more areas of the stack
After that: expand scope, influence architecture, and help raise the bar for the team
