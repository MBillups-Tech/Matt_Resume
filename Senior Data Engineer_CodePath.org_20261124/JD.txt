About the Role

Location: Remote (US)
Role Type: Full-Time
Reporting to: Head of Measurement, Evaluation & Learning
Compensation: $110,000 to $150,000 per year

We’re looking for a highly capable and pragmatic Senior Data Engineer who thrives in environments where they can take initiative, wear multiple hats, and turn ambiguity into action. This is a deeply hands-on role for someone eager to help shape our next-generation infrastructure. Data is the foundation of CodePath’s work, shaping how the organization serves learners, develops curriculum, and makes decisions. In this role, you will own the design of the architecture, pipelines, and models that power every critical function at CodePath, from student success analytics to business intelligence to AI-driven personalization. Because these systems hold student records, privacy and FERPA compliance are a central design consideration in all of this work.

As a Senior Data Engineer at CodePath, you will:

Help lead the design and development of a modern, scalable data platform that fuels analytics, data science, and machine learning
Work cross-functionally with engineers, data scientists, and business leaders on high-impact projects
Help build the foundation for data systems that directly enable student success, program outcomes, and organizational growth
This is a unique opportunity to:

Build something new: Design and build the next stage of CodePath’s data platform on top of the foundation we already have
Drive visibility and impact: Partner closely with leadership and cross-functional teams to deliver insights that shape our strategy
Scale CodePath’s mission: Ensure our 50,000+ learners and alumni (and growing) are supported with the infrastructure to thrive in the AI-native workforce
Every pipeline you build and every model you optimize will ripple across an organization supporting tens of thousands of students, alumni, learners, and partners, making this a unique opportunity to do data work with real-world impact. This is your chance to help design, build, scale, and address the systems that will serve as the backbone of CodePath’s future. Expect work that blends a mix of new projects and rebuilds of existing pipelines and models that require attention.

 

What You'll Do

Data Platform & Architecture: Design and maintain a scalable, modern data infrastructure (data warehouses, pipelines, data lakes, analytic products)
Pipelines & ETL/ELT: Build and optimize robust data workflows using SQL, dbt, Fivetran and other tools to deliver clean, reliable, and timely data
Data Ingestion: Bring in many new sources of data (such as our attendance bot, CredLens, Revelio, and the National Student Clearinghouse), migrate ingestion to Datastream, and build the monitoring we need now that Fivetran handles only a fraction of our ingestion
Modeling & Analytics Enablement: Create effective data models to power self-serve analytics, performance reporting, and strategic insights across teams
Business Intelligence: Develop and maintain dashboards and reporting tools (Hex or equivalent) that drive decision-making and clarity on key KPIs
AI-Ready Data Access: Enable secure and reliable use of our data by AI tools operated by non-technical stakeholders
LinkedIn Data Access: Enable cheap, safe, and reliable ad-hoc access to CodePath’s offline LinkedIn dataset
Governance, Privacy & Quality: Establish data quality and governance standards, and treat FERPA and student data privacy as a core design consideration, ensuring stakeholders can trust and act on data
Documentation & Maintainability: Document systems, models, and pipelines to support transparency, onboarding, and long-term scalability
Cross-Functional Collaboration: Work closely with engineers, data scientists, and business stakeholders to scope, prioritize, and deliver data solutions
Strategic Data Leadership: Partner with leadership to define and evolve CodePath’s data strategy, ensuring that infrastructure investments align with long-term organizational goals
Mentorship & Standards: Establish engineering best practices for data workflows, empower non-technical stakeholders to work confidently with data, and mentor and manage junior data engineers as the team grows
 

What Success Looks Like

Review and improve existing data models and schemas to support analytical and reporting needs
Share ownership of CodePath’s ETL pipeline and (BigQuery) data warehouse with the other data engineer; develop new data models (using dbt) to support evolving data needs across the organization
Share responsibility for CodePath’s Google Cloud Platform infrastructure (CloudRun, BigQuery, App Engine) to support data and dashboarding needs, including security and permissions, scalability, and uptime
Define a roadmap for data infrastructure that aligns with organizational growth and impact measurement goals
Propose and implement innovative solutions to improve data ingestion, processing, and storage
Develop and implement metrics to demonstrate that data engineering solutions align with business objectives and strategic goals
 

What We're Looking For

5+ years of experience in data engineering or similar roles
Strong SQL skills and experience building efficient pipelines, including performance tuning
Proven ability to work autonomously and deliver outcomes in a fast-changing environment
Expertise in building data pipelines end-to-end, from ingestion through transformation to final visualization. Deep understanding of data modeling principles (dimensional modeling, data normalization)
Hands-on experience managing cloud data warehouses (BigQuery, Snowflake, Databricks, or Redshift)
Proficiency with CodePath’s primary data stack, including BigQuery, Postgres, Fivetran, dbt, Hex, and Streamlit
Strong communicator who can make complex analysis clear and actionable for diverse stakeholders
 

Nice to Have

Expertise in, or strong interest in, one or more of the following: integration with AI agents, AI classification workflows and evals, and privacy and compliance
Experience developing custom data connectors and integrating third-party data sources such as the National Student Clearinghouse, Revelio, and CredLens
Familiarity with data quality frameworks and governance best practices
Familiarity with data privacy and security best practices
Background in a fast-paced, startup environment with the adaptability to thrive in ambiguity
 

Compensation

CodePath has standardized salaries based on the position’s level, no matter where you live. For this role, we’re hiring for a senior level position at an annual salary of $110,000 to $150,000. Salary is determined based on your relevant experience and skills as evaluated through our interview process.

 

Full-Time Employee Benefits

This is a remote position—work from anywhere in the US! CodePath prioritizes employee well-being with a competitive benefits package to support your health, financial security, and work-life balance.

Health & Wellness: Medical, dental, and vision insurance (90% employer-covered for employees and dependents), employer-funded healthcare reimbursement, FSAs, and Employee Assistance Program
Financial Security: 401(k), employer-paid life & disability insurance, and identity theft protection
Work-Life Balance: Generous PTO, paid holidays, 10 weeks of fully paid parental leave, and an annual year-end company closure (Dec 24 – Jan 2)
Professional Growth: $1,000 annual professional development stipend and home office setup support
​​Student Loan Forgiveness: CodePath is a qualifying employer for Public Service Loan Forgiveness (PSLF), helping employees manage student loan debt
Additional Perks: Pet wellness plans, legal services, home/auto insurance discounts, and exclusive marketplace savings
