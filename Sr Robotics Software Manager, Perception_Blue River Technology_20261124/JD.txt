Position Description
We’re Blue River, a team of innovators driven to create intelligent machinery that solves monumental problems for our customers. We empower our customers – farmers, construction crews, and foresters to implement safer and more sustainable solutions, driving increased profitability with less reliance on scarce labor. We believe that focusing on the small stuff, pixel-by-pixel and task-by-task, leads to big gains. 

Blue River Technology aligns with John Deere’s vision to “innovate on behalf of humanity” by quickly identifying and solving high-value, high-uncertainty challenges in AI, machine learning, computer vision, and robotics. BRT acts as a research and development flywheel, building not only new products but also new platforms that reliably create value for both Deere and its customers. From fully autonomous machines to highly precise farming equipment, BRT and Deere are partnering to deliver technical breakthroughs across industries such as agriculture and construction. 

Our people are at the heart of what we do. Through cross-disciplinary collaboration, this mission-driven team is eager to define the new frontier of robotics. We ask hard questions, iterate rapidly, and get our boots in the field to figure it out. We won’t give up until we’ve made a tangible and positive impact on the planet!

Blue River Technology is based in Santa Clara, CA. 

 

Summary

We are seeking a high-caliber Senior Robotics Software Manager to build, scale, and lead the engineering team responsible for delivering our production perception pipeline – including camera integration, model inference, runtime optimization, deployment, and spatial mapping. In this role, you will balance people leadership, architectural stewardship, and cross-functional execution to deploy intelligent systems reliably at scale. You will define the strategic technical direction for the perception pipeline and internal tooling, navigate architectural trade-offs, champion engineering rigor across test and release, and align with cross-functional leadership to drive predictable roadmap execution.

Beyond technical execution, you will serve as a culture carrier, operationalizing our core values and principles into daily practices while scaling a high-trust, inclusive, and performant team.

Employment Type: Full-Time
Work Location: Remote in the United States
Visa sponsorship: Open to visa sponsorship.
Job Responsibilities

A combination, not necessarily all-inclusive, of the following:

People Leadership & Talent Development
Build, coach, and retain a high-performing software engineering team through career development paths, regular feedback cycles, and proactive performance management.
Lead hiring and onboarding to scale perception engineering capabilities while fostering a culture of trust, learning, and inclusivity.
Technical Leadership 
Own the design, stability, observability, and evolution of the perception software pipeline. 
Champion engineering best practices for software development, system design, and code quality.
Establish data-driven mechanisms to track software stability, enforce rigorous testing and release standards, and systematically prioritize technical debt reduction.
Drive internal tooling and infrastructure improvements to enable faster experimentation and rapid iteration across the team.
Cross-Functional Collaboration
Partner closely with Core Software infrastructure to optimize real-time middleware, data-transport, and image pipelines for performance.
Collaborate with Systems, Data, CV/ML, QA, Hardware, and downstream planning/actuation teams to allocate capacity and deliver cohesive, mission-critical product milestones.
Clearly communicate architectural trade-offs and build consensus with cross-functional leadership to deliver on business outcomes.
Execution & Ownership
Dive in to unblock teams, resolve complex technical bottlenecks, and accelerate critical decision-making.
Lead the end-to-end delivery of initiatives from initial design through production deployment.
Apply a customer-first mindset to ensure solutions drive meaningful impact.
Required Experience and Skills

Leadership & Operational Execution:
3+ years of direct people management experience, including talent acquisition, onboarding, career progression, mentorship, and performance management.
Proven track record of driving efficient delivery using Agile methodologies (Scrum/Kanban) and tools like Jira (or equivalent) for capacity planning and roadmapping across complex cross-functional dependencies.
Demonstrated ability to operationalize leadership principles and core values into everyday decisions, standards, and team culture.
Technical Skills:
6+ years of hands-on software engineering experience in robotics, perception, embedded systems, or autonomous platforms, demonstrating technical depth and judgment of a senior/staff-level engineer.
Strong programming experience in C++ (14/17/20) for real-time systems, Python for tooling and automation.
Experience in modern test automation (e.g., simulation, HIL), chained CI/CD pipelines, and robust Git workflows.
General Skills:
Strong architectural judgment and problem-solving skills.
Excellent communication and collaboration abilities.
Preferred Experience and Skills

Experience with robotics middleware and real-time communication frameworks.
Hands-on experience combining camera streams with other sensors (such as LiDAR and IMUs) on embedded compute platforms (e.g., NVIDIA Jetson / Orin).
Experience leveraging simulated environments (e.g., Isaac Sim) and synthetic dataset pipelines for perception validation.
Exposure to data processing frameworks (e.g., Spark, Airflow, or similar).
Experience with safety and reliability standards, such as IEC 61508.
