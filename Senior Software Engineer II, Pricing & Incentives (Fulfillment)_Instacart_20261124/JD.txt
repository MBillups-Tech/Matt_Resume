Overview
We're hiring a Senior Software Engineer II to be the backend lead for Instacart's Pricing & Incentives team. The team is a mix of ML and backend engineers building the systems behind shopper pay and incentives. We use ML to make real-time, financially critical, compliance-sensitive decisions that keep shopper pay fair, protect contribution profit, and keep orders on time. We also own Shopper Incentives (getting shoppers online in the right place at the right time), Shopper Acquisition, and Tip Recommendations.

The team drives roughly a third of the company's annual contribution profit goal, so your work will show up directly in business results.

About the Job

Proactively identify gaps in system design, reliability, and correctness, and drive them to resolution
