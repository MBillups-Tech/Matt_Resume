Welcome’s take
With healthcare expenses notoriously high in the US, patients seek quality care that is cost-effective. This a challenge Strive Health is tackling head-on, aiming to provide affordable, value-based care that yields better patient outcomes and lower bills for patients with kidney disease.
 

Strive has built an integrated care delivery system for patients diagnosed with chronic or end-stage kidney disease. By employing machine learning algorithms to interpret patient data and predict disease progression, the company delivers targeted interventions that are both cheaper and more effective than current methods of detection and treatment.
 

Having raised a high degree of funding, Strive Health's trajectory appears promising. The company is leveraging this capital to scale its team, strengthen end-to-end strategic partnerships, and expand its multi-specialty service offerings. At the same time, Strive is enhancing its value-based care model through advanced technology, including AI-driven tools and analytics, to deliver more personalized, data-informed kidney care solutions.

You'll be working on
As a Staff Software Engineer in our Product & Technology organization, you will help define and evolve the technical strategy and architecture of Strive’s platform, ensuring engineering decisions align tightly with our clinical and business goals
This is a senior, hands-on individual contributor role. You will take on the most complex and ambiguous technical problems, designing and building scalable, reliable, and secure software while balancing the need to maintain and scale existing systems with the need to deliver new capabilities for patients, clinicians, and partners
Your impact will extend well beyond the systems you build yourself. You will help teams make sound architectural decisions, establish reusable patterns and standards, address systemic technical risk, and mentor engineers across multiple teams, raising the bar on design, implementation quality, and cross-functional collaboration
This role reports to a Senior Engineering Manager or Director of Engineering
Lead the technical strategy, architecture, and roadmap for one or more critical domains within Strive’s platform, grounded in our business objectives and long-term product vision
Design and deliver end-to-end solutions for complex, cross-team initiatives, from problem framing and architecture through implementation, rollout, and production operation
Stay hands-on in the code, writing high-quality, well-tested, observable software, especially where complexity, risk, or ambiguity calls for senior engineering leadership
Make pragmatic architectural tradeoffs that balance scalability, reliability, security, compliance, delivery speed, cost, and long-term maintainability, while keeping the platform simple for other engineers to build on
Design and evolve the APIs, services, data models, integrations, and distributed systems that support Strive’s products and healthcare operations
Partner with Product, Clinical, Data, Security, and Operations leaders to make sure we solve the right problems in the right order, with clear outcomes and measures of success
Identify systemic technical risks, architectural limitations, performance bottlenecks, and technical debt, and lead the work to address them
Establish reusable architectural patterns, reference implementations, and engineering standards that help teams build consistently and effectively
Drive platform-wide improvements in developer experience, reliability, observability, security posture, and performance that measurably improve how engineering delivers value
Provide technical leadership across teams by facilitating design and architecture reviews, guiding major engineering decisions, and unblocking teams through hands-on help
Participate in production support and incident response, helping teams find root causes and turn operational lessons into better architecture, tooling, and practices
Collaborate with Security and Compliance to ensure systems protect sensitive healthcare information and meet regulatory and organizational requirements
Mentor and coach engineers and tech leads through design discussions, code reviews, pairing, and day-to-day work, helping them strengthen their technical judgment and grow as owners of their domains
Evaluate emerging technologies, including AI-assisted engineering tools, and identify opportunities to improve developer productivity, software quality, and engineering effectiveness
Communicate engineering strategy, architectural decisions, risks, and tradeoffs clearly to technical and non-technical audiences, from engineering teams to executives
