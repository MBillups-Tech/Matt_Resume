Standard Job Description
Shape the architecture. Accelerate AI adoption. Build software that scales across missions.

The Lockheed Martin Artificial Intelligence Center (LAIC) is seeking an experienced Level 4 Staff A/AI Machine Learning Engineer to help drive the technical direction of Cognitive Baseline, a team focused on building reusable AI/ML capabilities, scalable software foundations, and modern developer tooling for mission critical applications.

Cognitive Baseline enables engineering teams across Lockheed Martin to discover, integrate, extend, and deploy software and AI capabilities more efficiently.

We’re looking for a technically strong engineer who can see beyond individual implementations to understand system-level architecture, identify design weaknesses, and guide teams toward sustainable solutions. This role requires someone who is equally comfortable designing software architectures, performing deep technical code reviews, mentoring engineers, and collaborating directly with mission teams to solve complex integration challenges.

The ideal candidate brings strong engineering judgment, a passion for software quality, and the ability to balance near-term delivery with long-term maintainability, extensibility, and mission value.

Key Responsibilities Include:

Lead the architecture, design, and development of reusable software frameworks, AI/ML tooling, and enterprise-scale engineering capabilities.
Establish and reinforce software engineering best practices, including modular design, separation of concerns, dependency inversion, testability, and maintainability.
Conduct in-depth merge request reviews that evaluate architectural integrity, code readability, abstraction boundaries, design patterns, and long-term sustainability.
Identify architectural gaps, technical debt, and opportunities to simplify or modernize existing software solutions.
Drive improvements to software development workflows, CI/CD pipeline, automated testing, and secure software delivery.
Basic Qualifications
Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science, Software Engineering, Computer Engineering, or a related technical discipline, or equivalent experience.
Advanced proficiency in Python, C++, and/or other modern programming languages.
Demonstrated experience designing and implementing complex software systems.
Strong understanding of software architecture, SOLID principles, design patterns, and modular development.
Experience with Git-based development, CI/CD pipelines, pipelines, automated testing, and DevSecOps practices.
Desired Skills
Experience architecting AI/ML platforms, MLOps frameworks, or reusable machine learning capabilities.
Strong C++ or python software architecture expertise, including packaging, dependency management, typing, and testing.
Experience with PyTorch, TensorFlow, model lifecycle management, or AI/ML deployment.
Experience with LLMs, agent orchestration, MCP, agentic workflows, or AI-enabled developer tooling.
Experience with Docker, Kubernetes, Linux, and containerized or air-gapped deployments.
Experience with GitLab CI/CD, secure software supply chains, and DevSecOps automation.
Experience refactoring tightly coupled or legacy systems into modular, maintainable architectures.
