Our company partner is seeking a Software Engineer to join their DevOps organization and help build the tools, automation, and platform capabilities that support modern software delivery.



This role is targeted toward a mid-level software engineer with established software development skills and hands-on exposure to DevOps, cloud, or platform engineering practices. You will design and develop internal tools, automation, integrations, and platform services that improve developer productivity, streamline software delivery, and enhance platform reliability.



Working with application, infrastructure, database, and security teams, you will help build scalable solutions that support the full software development lifecycle. The ideal candidate has experience developing applications, APIs, or automation solutions using modern software engineering practices and has worked with technologies such as CI/CD pipelines, cloud platforms, containerization, infrastructure automation, or Kubernetes in a development environment.



While the primary focus of this role is software engineering, successful candidates will bring an understanding of how modern applications are built, deployed, and operated. This position provides an opportunity to further develop platform engineering expertise while building software solutions that enable efficient, reliable, and scalable software delivery across the organization.
