SCADA Developer (Wonderware)

$120,000 - $140,000 + Performance Bonus + 401(k) + Medical, Dental & Vision + Long-Term Career Stability + Technical Development + PTO

United States (Remote)

Are you a Wonderware Developer looking to join a specialist systems integrator where you can build deep technical expertise while working on high-impact projects?

On offer is a long-term, stable position within an established industrial automation and SCADA business, delivering enterprise-level solutions for major industrial clients.

The company has built a strong reputation for high-quality technical delivery and, importantly, retaining staff between projects rather than laying people off when project work comes to an end. With the ability to cross-train onto other projects as priorities shift, you'll have the opportunity to continually develop your technical skillset while building a long-term career. Due to new project growth, they are now looking to add another SCADA specialist to their team.

This role would suit a mid-level Wonderware / SCADA Developer with Oil & Gas experience, looking for long-term development, technical progression and a stable home for their career.

The Role

Develop and support Wonderware / AVEVA SCADA systems across industrial projects
Work on greenfield projects, Wonderware migrations and integration of acquired assets
Troubleshoot and resolve break/fix and system integration issues
Work across a mix of streamlined and complex integration requirements
Remote position with minimal travel requirements

The Person

Experience working with Wonderware (ideally recent versions from 2020 onwards)
Strong SCADA development and industrial automation experience
Previous experience within the Oil & Gas industry
Comfortable working across development, integration and troubleshooting projects
